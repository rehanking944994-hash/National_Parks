Our Story

Protecting Pakistan's wild places, one park at a time

We're a small team of nature lovers mapping, documenting and celebrating the national parks, wildlife and landscapes that make Pakistan extraordinary.

Who we are

Built by explorers who wanted a better map

National Parks started as a weekend project: a simple list of the places we kept driving hours to reach, from the alpine meadows of the north to the wetlands and deserts further south. What began as a personal travel journal grew into a resource for anyone planning a trip into Pakistan's protected wilderness.

Today we bring together park information, wildlife profiles and travel tips in one place, so the next visitor spends less time searching and more time outdoors. We're not a government agency — just people who believe these places are worth knowing, and worth protecting.
